1. Executive Summary

JobTrack AI is an AI-powered job search and application platform built specifically for Indian professionals. It solves the core problem of wasted job applications by helping candidates know whether a job is worth applying to before they apply, and then helping them apply better when the answer is yes.

The platform is live at jobtrackai.co.in and operates on a credit-based freemium model where core features (CV parsing, job search, tracker) are free and AI-powered features consume credits.

2. Problem Statement

The Job Seeker Problem

  • Indian professionals apply to dozens of jobs with the same generic CV and get ghosted
  • No way to quickly assess if a job is a genuine fit before spending 30 minutes applying
  • Generic CVs get filtered by ATS before a human ever reads them
  • No structured interview preparation tailored to the specific role
  • Application tracking is done in Excel or not at all — people lose track of what they applied to

Market Gap

Existing tools (Naukri, LinkedIn, Shine) are job boards, not career intelligence tools. They show you jobs but give you no help deciding which to pursue or how to compete. Western tools (Resume.io, Kickresume, Rezi) exist but are not localised for Indian market rates, salary expectations, or career contexts.

4. Target Users

Primary Persona — Active Job Seeker

  • Experience: 2–10 years, mid to senior level
  • Location: Tier 1 Indian cities (Mumbai, Pune, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Delhi)
  • Actively applying to 5–20 jobs per month
  • Pain: Spending hours tailoring CVs manually, no feedback on fit
  • Willingness to pay: ₹200–500 for tools that give a clear edge

Secondary Persona — Passive Job Seeker

  • Employed but open to better opportunities
  • Wants to quickly assess inbound JDs without committing hours
  • Will use JD evaluation heavily, tailoring occasionally

Tertiary Persona — Recruiter (Future)

  • Talent acquisition professional at a company or staffing firm
  • Needs to quickly screen and shortlist candidates against a JD
  • Currently admin-only; roadmap feature for general availability

7. Constraints and Assumptions

Constraints

  • Infrastructure on free tiers — Render (backend), Vercel (frontend), Supabase (DB)
  • Single developer/founder — no dedicated engineering team
  • Razorpay account pending activation — payment collection not yet live
  • Anthropic API Tier 1 — 50 RPM limit on Sonnet 4.6

Assumptions

  • Indian professionals are willing to pay small amounts for clear career ROI
  • Word-of-mouth from a strong product will be the primary acquisition channel initially
  • Credit model is more acceptable than subscriptions at early stage
  • Render free tier is sufficient until 200+ active users

8. Success Criteria

  • User completes full cycle (CV upload → JD eval → Tailor → Interview prep) in under 5 minutes
  • JD evaluation grade is consistent and trusted by users
  • Tailored CV passes ATS screening at higher rates than generic CV
  • Interview prep answers are grounded in the user's actual CV, not generic advice
  • Zero data leaks — each user only sees their own data
